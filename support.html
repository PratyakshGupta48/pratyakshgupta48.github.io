DOT Wallet is a self-custody iOS wallet for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana. Your recovery phrase is the only way to restore access. We cannot reset passwords or move funds for you.

Lost recovery phrase

If you lose your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase and have no backup, Mettle Labs cannot recover your wallet or funds. There is no company-held copy of your keys.

How to back up

  • Write down your recovery phrase offline and store it safely. Never share it.
  • Optionally use passkey-encrypted backup in Settings (device must support passkeys). This encrypts your phrase with a passkey; it is not a cloud “forgot password” service operated by us.
  • You can also import your phrase into another BIP39-compatible wallet you trust.

Common questions

  • No account: DOT Wallet has no login. Access is the phrase (and optional Face ID app lock on this iPhone).
  • Wrong network: Only send an asset on its matching network (BTC, ETH, or SOL mainnet as shown in the app).
  • Privacy: See Privacy Policy. We do not run analytics or hold your balances on a DOT Wallet server.
