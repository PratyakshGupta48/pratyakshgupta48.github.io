Privacy Policy

DOT Wallet · Operated by Mettle Labs Private Limited · Last updated: 8 October 2026

DOT Wallet is a self-custody iOS wallet for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana. It is not affiliated with Polkadot’s DOT ticker and does not support Polkadot.

What we do not collect

There is no DOT Wallet account, no DOT Wallet product backend that stores your balances or recovery phrase, and no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK in the current app. The iOS privacy manifest declares no tracking.

Keys and data on your device

  • Your BIP39 recovery phrase (seed) and address-book nicknames/addresses are stored in the iOS Keychain on this iPhone (this-device-only accessibility).
  • Non-secret preferences (for example appearance, currency, hide-balances, recovery-verified, app-lock timing) may use UserDefaults on device.
  • DOT Wallet never uploads your recovery phrase to a DOT Wallet server — there is no such server API for custody.

Face ID / Touch ID

LocalAuthentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode) can gate revealing the recovery phrase, confirming sends, and optional app lock. Biometric templates stay in the system Secure Enclave; DOT Wallet does not receive Face ID imagery.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan recipient addresses when sending. Receive QR codes are generated on device (not scanned from the photo library). Microphone and photo-library access are not requested.

Network requests (public chain infrastructure)

To show balances, prices, activity, and to broadcast signed transactions, the app makes HTTPS calls to public third-party RPC and explorer endpoints. Those operators (not Mettle Labs) may see public addresses, your IP address, and transaction payloads you choose to broadcast. Typical defaults (subject to change in the app) include:

  • Ethereum: ethereum.publicnode.com, eth.blockscout.com
  • Bitcoin: blockstream.info, mempool.space
  • Solana: solana-rpc.publicnode.com, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com
  • Fiat spot prices: api.coinbase.com

Optional passkey-encrypted backup uses Apple’s AuthenticationServices / platform passkeys with relying party ID pratyakshgupta48.github.io. Ciphertext for that feature stays in Keychain unless you export it; passkey material is managed by Apple’s passkey system (for example iCloud Keychain when you use a synced passkey).

Clipboard

Addresses and (if you choose) recovery words may be copied to the system pasteboard with timed clearing to reduce shoulder-surf risk. Clipboard contents are not sent to a DOT Wallet server.

Children

DOT Wallet is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If product behaviour changes (for example adding analytics or a hosted account), this policy will be updated and the in-app privacy summary should be updated to match.
